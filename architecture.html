The 4 Golden Rules of African Medical Sub-Contracting

The commercial guidelines that drive 80%+ reply rates when pitching winning distributors.

RULE 1
Never Bid Directly

Local procurement acts legally favor domestic citizen-owned entities. Partner with the winner rather than spending \$20k+ on foreign bid securities.

RULE 2
Strike in Month 0

Between Day 14 and Day 30, distributors are choosing factories before wiring their 30% advance deposit. Once the wire clears, the deal is locked.

RULE 3
Always Quote CIF Border

Distributors dislike navigating ocean and rail freight tariffs. Quote CIF Songwe or CIF Mwanza Border so their landed unit cost is crystal clear.

RULE 4
Match 8-Digit HS Codes

African customs authorities aggressively inspect tariff codes. Matching their exact historical HS lines (e.g. 9018.39.00) ensures zero clearance hold-ups.

Maritime & Overland Clearance Corridors to Landlocked Malawi

Why ocean shipping + overland haulage necessitates the strict 30-day "Month 0" factory deposit window.

Northern Corridor (Dar es Salaam) 58% Central Malawi Inflow

Route: Ocean freight from Ningbo/Shanghai ➔ Port of Dar es Salaam (Tanzania) ➔ TanZam rail/road ➔ Songwe Border Post (Kasumulu) ➔ Lilongwe central depots.

• Transit Time: 22 days sea + 10 days port dwell + 6 days trucking = 38–48 Days
• Typical Cargo: Chinese hospital furniture, CE-certified IV cannulas, PPE.
Southern Corridor (Port of Beira) 52% Southern Region Inflow

Route: Ocean freight from Nhava Sheva (India) / Durban ➔ Port of Beira (Mozambique) ➔ Tete corridor ➔ Mwanza Border Post ➔ Blantyre referral hospitals.

• Transit Time: 18 days sea + 8 days port dwell + 4 days trucking = 30–42 Days
• Typical Cargo: Indian generic formulations, antibiotics, surgical suture packs.
